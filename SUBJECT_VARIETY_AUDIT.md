# Subject repetition and variety improvements

The previous site assigned **all 440 Learnova companion-exam questions to the catchall `Companion Practice Exams` subject**. This was one cause of repetitive subject names.

This update:

1. Matches companion-exam question text to the main Learnova book only when normalized question text agrees exactly: **221 matched**, assigned corresponding source-based subjects/subtopics.
2. Leaves **219 unmatched companion-exam problems** in the clearly marked `Mixed practice — topic unclassified` group instead of guessing their subjects. Those items remain browseable in the library by source.
3. Adds separate subject and subtopic selection to practice and library filters.
4. Uses a round-robin-like **balanced subject sampler**, with subtopic diversification, for mixed sessions. In the isolated browser test, one 20-question source-key session included **14 distinct civil subjects**, with no adjacent repeated subject; one 110-question source-key session did the same. Random draws may differ.
5. Keeps all 2,294 indexed records, all 65 diagrams, six PDFs, and review-only status labels.

Important: The 91 manually checked questions are disproportionately concentrated in a subset of subjects. Subject balancing cannot create checked questions where the review bank has none. Balanced variety is **not** the official NCEES distribution. It also doesn't guarantee that conceptually similar problems in the source books are distinct.
