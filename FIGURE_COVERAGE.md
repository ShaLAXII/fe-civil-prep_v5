# Diagrams and sketches — complete source access

Every figure actually printed on every page of the six source PDFs is accessible inside the website through original-page images. This includes trusses, beams, foundations, survey figures, construction schedules, cross sections, hydraulic plots, tables and equations. The main two books and four Learnova companion exams are available in **Original books + diagrams**.

The additional 65 question-linked SVG cards are selective redrawings, not all figures independently reproduced as editable drawings. The `DIAGRAM_AUDIT.csv` records their provenance and fidelity. Because the originals are also included, users can compare every redrawing with the source rather than trust a schematic that might omit geometry or labels.

Original-page images are compressed WebP renderings at 118 dpi, not embedded PDF viewer frames. Use the zoom and next/previous-page controls. For maximum original resolution, consult the user's source PDFs directly. Original publisher errors remain possible.
